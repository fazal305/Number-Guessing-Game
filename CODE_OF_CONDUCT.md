# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, give and accept constructive feedback gracefully, and keep discussion focused on the work. Harassment, insults, and personal or political attacks are not tolerated in issues, pull requests, or any other project space.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, email **fazalabbas2002@gmail.com**. Reports are read privately and handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove comments, commits, or contributions that violate this code, and may block repeat offenders.
